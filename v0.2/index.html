All 131 events

Events and captions transcribed from the chart “The Dow Jones Industrial Average: 1896–2016” by VirtueofSelfishInvesting.com. Events 122–131 (2017–2026) continue it in the same style; 2026 covers the year to date.

Prices are monthly closes. The Dow comes from MeasuringWorth's daily series through 1991 (years before 1915 are scaled to the official year-end closes) and from Yahoo Finance since 1992; the S&P 500, Nasdaq, Nikkei 225, FTSE 100 and DAX come from Yahoo Finance. The Mag 7 line is an equal-weighted, never-rebalanced basket of the seven stocks' dividend-adjusted prices, starting at 100 in December 2015. The latest month is month to date. Recovery periods mark how long the Dow took to regain a prior peak, as shown on the original chart.

Archive images come from Wikimedia Commons and are public domain or Creative Commons licensed. Each one shows its author and license, with a link to its Commons page.

Presidential portraits come from Wikimedia Commons. All are public domain, mostly official White House or Library of Congress portraits, except Franklin D. Roosevelt's 1944 portrait by Leon Perskie (CC BY 2.0).
